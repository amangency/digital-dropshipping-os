## 🧪 MONDAY LAUNCH: HOW TO DEPLOY THESE 3 IN ADS MANAGER

### Architecture (Inside the Same Duplicated Ad Set):
```
Ad Set: Interests (Women 25-42, Motherhood/Parenting) — ₹500/day ABO
├── Ad 1: Video Ad 3 (Pain Angle — Proven Winner, 11-14% CTR)
├── Ad 2: IMAGE AD 1 (Half-Eaten Dabba — Pain/Guilt)
├── Ad 3: IMAGE AD 2 (Weekly Calendar — Desire/Speed)
└── Ad 4: IMAGE AD 3 (Immunity Shield — Fear/Health)
```

### Why This Structure Works:
1. **Format Diversity**: Meta gets both Video + Image formats. Algorithm can serve images to fast-scrollers and video to engaged viewers.
2. **Angle Diversity**: Pain + Desire + Fear — all 3 core emotional triggers covered.
3. **Algorithmic Budget Allocation**: Meta will auto-distribute budget to whichever format/angle produces the lowest CPP.
4. **Lower Blended CPM**: Static images typically deliver 30-50% cheaper impressions, pulling down the ad set's average CPM.

### Testing Rules:
- Give each image ad **minimum ₹200–₹300 spend** (at least 10-15 visitors) before judging.
- If an image ad generates **Link CTR > 3%** and **Cost per LPV < ₹30**, it's a KEEPER.
- If an image ad matches or beats Video Ad 3's conversion rate, **DUPLICATE and scale it immediately** — images are 10x easier to create variations of.
