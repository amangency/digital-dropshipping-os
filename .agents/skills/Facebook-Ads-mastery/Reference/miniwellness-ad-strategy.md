### Key Findings (DATA-PROVEN, NOT THEORY):

```
1. PRODUCT DEMAND IS 100% VALIDATED (5.70% CVR)
   → Cold traffic conversion rate of 5.70% is extraordinary (normal is 2-3%)
   → 23 moms willingly paid ₹499 to a stranger on the internet
   → PRODUCT IS NOT THE PROBLEM — Delivery & Funnel economics were the bottleneck

2. THE ROOT CAUSE OF HIGH CPP (₹405):
   → CPP = CPC (₹20.35) / CVR (5.70%) = ~₹357 - ₹405
   → The CVR was amazing, but CPC was inflated because:
     a) CPM was ₹527 due to narrow city radius pins (+25mi / +50mi filters)
     b) Video retention was low (30s script, feature dump, 0 social proof)

3. WHY ZERO PROFIT WAS FELT (The Real Mistake):
   → Spend: ₹9,322 | Revenue: ₹11,477 (23 x ₹499) | Gross Profit: ₹2,155 (ROAS 1.23x)
   → Without Order Bumps (₹199/₹149) or Upsells (₹499), AOV stayed flat at ₹499
   → With Bumps + Upsell, AOV would be ₹650+ → Revenue ₹14,950 → Profit ₹5,628 (ROAS 1.6x)

4. VIDEO ADS > IMAGE ADS (100% Confirmed):
   → Video ads generated ALL 23 sales; Image ads generated ZERO sales.
```

### 4 Mistakes to NEVER Repeat:
```
❌ MISTAKE #1: Selling front-end alone with NO Order Bumps or Upsells.
❌ MISTAKE #2: Adding narrow +25mi/+50mi city radius pins (inflates CPM to ₹500+).
❌ MISTAKE #3: Running short 30s feature-list videos with 0 social proof.
❌ MISTAKE #4: Killing cheap CPC ad sets early (Ad Set 3 had ₹12.61 CPC but was killed at ₹252).
```
