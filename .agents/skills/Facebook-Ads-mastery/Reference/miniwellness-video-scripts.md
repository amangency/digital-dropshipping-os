## RECORDING TIPS:

```
✅ Record in good natural light (near a window)
✅ Phone horizontal or vertical — vertical preferred for Reels/Stories
✅ Look directly into the camera lens (not the screen)
✅ Speak naturally — like talking to a friend, not reading
✅ Keep energy HIGH in first 3 seconds (hook decides everything)
✅ Show the product on screen when listing features (phone mockup)
✅ Add captions/subtitles in post-production (many watch on mute)
✅ Background: Kitchen or clean home setting (relatable for moms)

❌ Don't read from a script visibly — memorize the hook at minimum
❌ Don't use complicated words — "5th standard English" rule
❌ Don't rush through the proof section — let testimonials breathe
❌ Don't forget to smile when talking about the solution
```
