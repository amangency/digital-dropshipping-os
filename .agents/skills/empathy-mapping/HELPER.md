# 🗺️ Empathy Mapping & Customer Research — HELPER GUIDE

## Ye Skill Kya Hai?

Ye ek **complete customer research system** hai jo Google Sheet "Empathy Mapping - Apne Customer Ko Samajhna" se extract kiya gaya hai. Iska kaam hai tumhe apne **customer ko itna achha samajhna** ki tumhari ads, landing page, aur offer — sab UNKI language me bole, unki pain ko address kare, aur unhe feel ho ki "ye toh mere baare me bol raha hai!"

---

## 🎯 Tum Is Skill Se Kya-Kya Kar Sakte Ho?

### 1. 🧠 Customer Avatar Banana
**Kya milega:** Tumhare ideal buyer ka poora profile — naam, age, job, daily routine, pains, desires sab.

**Kaise use karo:**
> "Mere product ka customer avatar bana do — mera product [PRODUCT] hai jo [AUDIENCE] ke liye hai"

---

### 2. 🗺️ Empathy Map Fill Karna
**Kya milega:** 5 dimensions (Thinking, Feeling, Seeing, Saying, Hearing) me tumhare buyer ki complete mapping.

**Kaise use karo:**
> "Meri niche [XYZ] hai, iska Empathy Map fill kar do"

---

### 3. 📊 Market Sophistication & Awareness Level Jaanna
**Kya milega:** Eugene Schwartz ke according tumhara market kis level par hai — aur accordingly kaise messaging karni chahiye.

**Kaise use karo:**
> "Mera product [XYZ] hai — market ka sophistication level kya hoga aur buyer awareness level kya hai?"

---

### 4. 😰 20 Pain Points Nikalna
**Kya milega:** Tumhare audience ki 20 specific, deep, real pains — jo woh daily feel karte hain.

**Kaise use karo:**
> "Mere audience ki 20 deepest pains nikaal do — niche hai [XYZ]"

---

### 5. 🪝 5 Type Ke Hooks Banana
**Kya milega:** Har ek pain point ko 5 alag-alag hook formats me convert karna:
- ✅ Pain Alignment Questions ("Are you tired of...?")
- ✅ Desired State Questions ("How would you like to...?")  
- ✅ First Person Direct ("What do I have to do to...?")
- ✅ If/Then Statements ("If you're wondering..., then...")
- ✅ Feeling Statements ("Do you feel...?")

**Kaise use karo:**
> "Meri niche [XYZ] hai — 20 pain points ke hooks bana do saare 5 formats me"

---

### 6. 📱 Image Ad Briefs Banana (5 Ads)
**Kya milega:** 5 unique image ad briefs — har ek me Headline + Supporting Line + CTA.

**Kaise use karo:**
> "Mujhe 5 image ad briefs chahiye apne product [XYZ] ke liye"

---

### 7. 🎬 Video Ad Scripts Banana (90 Seconds, Hinglish)
**Kya milega:** 5 video ad scripts jo:
- Hinglish me ho
- Ek person ke liye ho (talking to camera / voiceover)
- 90 seconds ka ho
- HOOK → AGITATE → REVEAL → PROOF → OFFER → CTA structure follow kare

**Kaise use karo:**
> "Mere product [XYZ] ke liye 5 Hinglish video ad scripts likh do — 90 seconds wale"

---

### 8. 🔢 Marketing Message Formula Se Ads Banana (M = (V-C) × A)
**Kya milega:** 3 formula-based ads jisme Value, Cost, aur Acceptability ke 10 components use hote hain.

**Kaise use karo:**
> "M = (V-C) × A formula use karke mere product ke 3 ads generate kar do"

---

### 9. 📝 Landing Page Copy Generate Karna (11 Sections)
**Kya milega:** Complete landing page copy — section by section:
1. Headlines (Main + Sub + Supporting)
2. Benefits Section
3. Before/After (6 + 6)
4. Uniqueness / USP
5. Testimonials (Social Proof)
6. Urgency Section
7. Trust/Emotion Section
8. Closing CTA (3 Doubt Eliminators)
9. FAQs
10. Product Description (Detailed)
11. 4 Bonus Ideas

**Kaise use karo:**
> "Mere product [XYZ] ki complete landing page copy generate kar do — sabhi 11 sections"

---

### 10. 🎯 Winning Message Dhundhna (9 Motivations Se)
**Kya milega:** 11 questions answer karke tumhe EK dominant message milega jo tumhari SAARI marketing ka center hoga.

**Kaise use karo:**
> "Mere product [XYZ] ke liye winning message dhundh do — 9 buying motivations check karke"

---

### 11. 💡 Belief Shift Mapping (Offer Ke Liye)
**Kya milega:** 6 key beliefs identify hogi jo customer ke mind me shift honi chahiye BEFORE woh buy kare.

**Kaise use karo:**
> "Mere product ke liye belief shift map kar do — customer ko kya believe karna hoga buying se pehle?"

---

### 12. 🔍 Awareness Level Deep Dive (5 Levels × 9 Questions)
**Kya milega:** Har awareness level ke liye buyer ki complete profile — kahan hangout karte hain, kya frustrations hain, kya language use karte hain, daily life kaisi hai.

**Kaise use karo:**
> "Mere niche [XYZ] ka awareness level deep dive kar do — saare 5 levels ke liye"

---

## ⚡ Quick Start — Naye Product Ke Liye Full Research Kaise Karein?

Agar tum ek NAYE product ke liye scratch se research karna chahte ho, toh ye order follow karo:

```
Step 1: "Mera product [XYZ] hai, target audience [ABC] — Customer Avatar bana do"
    ↓
Step 2: "Market sophistication aur awareness level bata do"
    ↓
Step 3: "20 deep pain points nikaal do"
    ↓
Step 4: "Saare 5 hook formats me convert kar do"
    ↓
Step 5: "Winning message dhundh do (9 motivations se)"
    ↓
Step 6: "Belief shift map kar do"
    ↓
Step 7: "5 image ad briefs bana do"
    ↓
Step 8: "5 video ad scripts likh do (Hinglish, 90 sec)"
    ↓
Step 9: "Complete landing page copy generate kar do"
    ↓
Step 10: "Empathy Map fill kar do final summary ke saath"
```

> 💡 **Pro Tip**: Ye saara kaam ek hi conversation me karo — taaki AI ke paas poora context rahe aur outputs ek dusre se connected rahen!

---

## ❌ Kya NAHI Karna Chahiye

| Galti | Kyu Galat Hai |
|-------|--------------|
| Research skip karke seedha ad banana | Ads guesswork ban jaate hain — paise waste |
| Sirf 3-4 pain points likhna | Minimum 20 chahiye — depth matters |
| Hook sirf ek format me banana | 5 formats = 5 alag angles = better testing |
| Landing page copy bina avatar ke likhna | Copy generic hogi — convert nahi karegi |
| Awareness level ignore karna | "Buy Now" ad unaware audience ko dikhaoge = zero sales |
| Winning message nahi dhundhna | Bina clear message ke marketing scattered hoti hai |
