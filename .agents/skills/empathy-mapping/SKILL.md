## MODULE 1: EMPATHY MAPPING GRID

### The 5 Dimensions of Customer Understanding

For any target audience, you MUST map these 5 dimensions:

| Dimension | What to Capture | Example Questions |
|-----------|----------------|-------------------|
| **THINKING** | Beliefs, assumptions, mental models, internal dialogue | "Kya sochta hai woh apne baare me?", "Uski sabse badi assumption kya hai?" |
| **FEELING** | Fears, frustrations, hopes, anxieties, desires | "Kis cheez se sabse zyada darta hai?", "Kya feel karta hai raat ko soye se pehle?" |
| **SEEING** | Environment, competitor solutions, social media feeds, peer results | "Uske around kya ho raha hai?", "Social media par kya dikhai deta hai usse?" |
| **SAYING** | Public statements, words used in conversations, complaints shared | "Doston se kya kehta hai?", "WhatsApp groups me kya likhta hai?" |
| **HEARING** | Advice from family/friends, society's expectations, influencer opinions | "Family kya bolti hai usse?", "Society ka kya pressure hai?" |

### Supporting Avatar Data Points
Before mapping, capture these foundational data points:

1. **Demographics**: Age range, gender, location, income level, education, job title
2. **Psychographics**: Values, personality traits, lifestyle, attitudes, opinions
3. **Interests**: Hobbies, media consumption, platforms used, influencers followed
4. **Affiliations**: Communities, groups, forums, professional networks
5. **Other**: Daily routine, buying habits, technology comfort, language preferences

> **Rule**: Empathy Map INCOMPLETE hai agar in 5 dimensions + supporting data points ke bina banaaya gaya. Har ek dimension me minimum 5 specific entries honi chahiye.

## MODULE 2: CUSTOMER AVATAR & MARKET RESEARCH PROMPTS

### Phase 1: Customer Avatar Creation

**Prompt #1 — Initial Avatar Discovery**
```
I have a product that [DESCRIBE WHAT YOUR PRODUCT DOES]. Please give me demographics, psychographics, and pain points of the ideal future customer including age range, job titles and common interests.

Also my target audience is INDIA.
```

**Prompt #2 — Avatar Condensation**
```
Can you please condense the above information into a valid customer avatar complete with name, age, job title — go as much detailed as you can and give me a detailed information about my audience.
```

### Phase 2: Market Sophistication & Awareness Level

**Prompt #3 — Eugene Schwartz Market Sophistication**
```
According to Eugene Schwartz what is level of sophistication for the market surrounding this customer avatar and product.
```

**Prompt #4 — Buyer Awareness Level**
```
Based upon Schwartz's level of awareness, what level of awareness does the ideal future customer present.
```

### EUGENE SCHWARTZ'S 5 AWARENESS LEVELS (Reference)

| Level | Name | Description | Marketing Approach |
|-------|------|-------------|-------------------|
| 1 | **Problem Unaware** | Customer doesn't even know they have a problem | Educate first, then agitate |
| 2 | **Problem Aware** | Knows the problem, doesn't know solutions exist | Name their pain precisely |
| 3 | **Solution Aware** | Knows solutions exist, doesn't know YOUR product | Differentiate, show mechanism |
| 4 | **Product Aware** | Knows your product, hasn't decided yet | Overcome objections, show proof |
| 5 | **Most Aware** | Ready to buy, just needs a push | Offer, urgency, CTA |

> **Critical Rule**: Your ad messaging MUST match the awareness level. Sending "Buy Now" to a Problem Unaware audience = wasted ad spend.

### Phase 3: Deep Pain Mining (Empathy Extraction)

**Prompt #5 — Pain Discovery (20 Points)**
```
What are some painful things that the above avatar might be seeing, saying, hearing, and thinking? Please list twenty things — be very specific as this will help me create my marketing message more stronger and connecting with my audience.
```

**Prompt #6 — Pain Alignment / Obvious Yes Questions**
```
Convert each of the above into obvious yes pain alignment questions.
```
> Example: "Are you tired of hearing these comments from your in-laws and family members that you are not raising your kid properly?"

**Prompt #7 — Desired Future State Questions**
```
Convert each of the above into an obvious Yes Desired State Question.
```
> Example: "How would you like to know the exact next steps to take in order to raise confident, well-mannered, strong kids that you can be proud of?"

**Prompt #8 — First Person Direct Questions**
```
Convert each of the above into First Person Direct Question.
```
> Example: "What do I have to do in order to make my ads perform better than my competitors?"

**Prompt #9 — If/Then Statements**
```
Convert each of the above into a If/Then Statement.
```
> Example: "If you are wondering how to get Insane ROI on every ad campaign you launch from the word go, then you have met me at the right time."

**Prompt #10 — Feeling Discovery**
```
List down 5 things that the above avatar might be feeling.
```

### THE 5 HOOK CONVERSION FORMATS

| Format | Structure | Use Case |
|--------|-----------|----------|
| **Pain Alignment** | "Are you tired of [specific pain]?" | Opening hooks for ads, starting copy |
| **Desired State** | "How would you like to [specific outcome]?" | Mid-copy, benefit sections |
| **First Person Direct** | "What do I have to do to [achieve result]?" | Headlines, internal dialogue matching |
| **If/Then Statement** | "If you are wondering [pain/desire], then [your solution]" | Ad hooks, email subject lines, video intros |
| **Feeling Statement** | "Do you feel [specific emotion]?" | Emotional connection, storytelling hooks |

> **Rule**: Run prompts #5 through #10 IN ORDER. Each prompt builds on the previous one's output. Skipping steps = weaker hooks.

## MODULE 3: AD CREATION SYSTEM

### Part A: Marketing Message Formula (M = (V - C) × A)

**The Formula:**
```
M = Marketing Message
V = Value
C = Cost
A = Acceptability
```

**Value Components:**
| Component | Core Question |
|-----------|--------------|
| **Appeal** | "How much do I want this?" |
| **Exclusivity** | "Where else can I get this?" |
| **Clarity** | "Do I really understand this?" |
| **Incentive** | "What else is in it for me?" |

**Cost Components:**
| Component | Core Question |
|-----------|--------------|
| **Mental Cost** | "How difficult will this be?" |
| **Fiscal Cost** | "How much money must I exchange for this?" |
| **Time Cost** | "How long/complicated is this going to be?" |

**Acceptability Components:**
| Component | Core Question |
|-----------|--------------|
| **Credibility** | "How much do I believe you?" |
| **Affinity** | "How much do I like you?" |
| **Disruption** | "How well does this garner my attention?" |

### Ad Structure Using M = (V-C) × A Formula

**Prompt #11 — Generate 3 Formula-Based Ads**
```
From the above context, I want to create an Ad which is based on the formula:

M = (V - C) A

[Include all 10 components as defined above]

My Ad is based on the format below:

[Hook] - I am going to share with you
Section 1 - [A Random Selection 1 out of the 10 components above not more than 40 words]
Section 2 - [A Random Selection 1 out of the 10 components above not more than 40 words]
Section 3 - [A Random Selection 1 out of the 10 components above not more than 40 words]
[CTA] - I am going to give you that.

Now use the above Formula to generate the 3 Ads for me.

Hook - [YOUR HOOK]
CTA - [YOUR CTA]
```

> **Note**: ONLY USE THIS PROMPT WHEN YOU HAVE TO CREATE ADS — not for general copy.

### VIDEO AD SCRIPT STRUCTURE (90-Second Framework)

| Timing | Section | Purpose | Duration |
|--------|---------|---------|----------|
| 0-3 sec | **HOOK** | Pattern interrupt — deepest pain or bold claim | 3 sec |
| 3-15 sec | **AGITATE** | Expand the pain, make them feel it | 12 sec |
| 15-35 sec | **REVEAL** | Introduce your solution/mechanism | 20 sec |
| 35-60 sec | **PROOF/BENEFITS** | Show what's inside, what they get, results | 25 sec |
| 60-75 sec | **OFFER** | Price, bonuses, value stack | 15 sec |
| 75-90 sec | **CTA + URGENCY** | Clear action + limited time/quantity | 15 sec |

## MODULE 5: MESSAGING STRATEGY (The Winning Message)

### 9 Core Buying Motivations

People buy because they want to:

| # | Motivation | Category |
|---|-----------|----------|
| 1 | **Make Money** | Gain |
| 2 | **Save Money** | Preservation |
| 3 | **Save Time** | Efficiency |
| 4 | **Avoid Effort** | Comfort |
| 5 | **Escape Mental or Physical Pain** | Pain Avoidance |
| 6 | **Get More Comfort** | Pleasure |
| 7 | **Achieve Greater Cleanliness/Hygiene/Health** | Wellbeing |
| 8 | **Gain Praise / Feel More Loved** | Emotional |
| 9 | **Increase Popularity / Social Status** | Status |

### 11 Questions to Find the ONE Winning Message

Answer ALL 11 questions for your specific product:

1. What are **five ways** my product/service will help them **make money**?
2. How can my product/service help them **save money** over the next week, month, or year?
3. How much **time** can I save them and what else could they do with that time?
4. What is something they **don't have to do anymore** once they get my product? *(This is how you figure out "avoid effort")*
5. What **physical pain** do I eliminate for them and what does that mean for their life/business?
6. How does my product **eliminate mental pain or worry** for them?
7. What are three ways my product can help them feel more **comfortable**?
8. How does my product help them achieve greater **cleanliness or hygiene**?
9. How does my product help them feel more **healthy or more alive**?
10. What are three ways my product is going to help them be the **envy of their friends** and feel **more loved** by their family?
11. How will buying my product make them feel more **popular** and increase their **social status**?

> **The Goal**: After answering all 11 questions, identify the ONE message that is the most emotionally powerful. THIS becomes the spearhead of ALL your marketing — ads, landing page, emails, everything.

> **Rule**: Most products have 2-3 dominant motivations. Pick the STRONGEST one as your lead message, use the others as supporting points.

## MODULE 6: BIG OFFER BELIEF SHIFT FRAMEWORK

### 6 Belief Questions Every Offer Must Answer

Before your prospect will buy, they must believe certain things. Map these 6 belief shifts:

| # | Question | Purpose |
|---|----------|---------|
| 1 | What do your prospects need to **BELIEVE** in order to take the action you need them to take to buy? | Identify core beliefs required |
| 2 | What do they need to **REALIZE**? (the "ah-ha" moments) | Map epiphany moments |
| 3 | What do they need to **AGREE** in advance? | Pre-frame agreements |
| 4 | What do you need to **SAY** in order for them to be **PRE-SOLD** on what you have? (By reframing their POV) | Reframe their perspective |
| 5 | What do you need to say for them to **ACCEPT** the need for what you have? | Build necessity |
| 6 | What do you need to say to **CHANNEL their desire** so they want it more than anything else? | Amplify desire |

### Webinar/Registration Variant
Same 6 questions but reframed for webinar/free registration funnel:

1. What do avatars need to **BELIEVE** to register for the webinar?
2. What do avatars need to **REALIZE** (ah-ha moments) before registering?
3. What do avatars need to **AGREE** on in advance to register?
4. What do you need to say for them to be **PRE-SOLD** on what's coming?
5. What do you need to say to **CHANNEL** their desire so they want it more than anything?

> **Rule**: Every piece of content in your funnel (ad → landing page → sales page → webinar → close) should systematically move the buyer through these 6 belief shifts. If ANY belief is unaddressed, conversion drops.

## MODULE 7: AWARENESS-LEVEL DEEP DIVE (KNOW MORE)

### 9 Questions Per Awareness Level

For EACH of the 5 Schwartz awareness levels, answer these 9 questions about your dream buyer:

1. Where does your dream buyer **hang out and congregate** on social media platforms?
2. Where does your dream buyer **get their information**?
3. What are their **5 biggest frustrations and challenges**?
4. What are their **5 hopes, 5 dreams, and 5 desires**?
5. What are their **5 biggest fears**?
6. What is their **preferred form of communication**?
7. What **5 phrases, exact language, and vernacular** do they use?
8. What does a **day in your dream buyer's life** look like?
9. What **makes them happy**?

### How to Use This Matrix

| Awareness Level | Primary Ad Strategy | Content Type |
|----------------|-------------------|-------------|
| **Problem Unaware** | Educational content → soft CTA | Blog posts, videos, "Did you know..." |
| **Problem Aware** | Pain agitation → introduce solution category | Problem-focused ads, if/then hooks |
| **Solution Aware** | Mechanism differentiation → why YOUR way is better | Comparison posts, case studies |
| **Product Aware** | Social proof + objection handling → offer | Testimonials, detailed benefits, FAQ |
| **Most Aware** | Direct offer + urgency + bonus stack | Flash sales, limited time, "Buy now" |

> **Rule**: Fill this matrix for your SPECIFIC niche before writing ANY ad copy. This is the FOUNDATION of all messaging. Without this, your ads are guesswork.

## EVALUATION CRITERIA

When reviewing a user's customer research, check:

1. **Empathy Map Completeness** — All 5 dimensions filled? Minimum 5 entries each?
2. **Avatar Specificity** — Can you NAME the person? Age, job, daily routine?
3. **Pain Depth** — Surface-level pains or deep emotional triggers?
4. **Hook Variety** — All 5 formats created? (Pain, Desired State, First Person, If/Then, Feeling)
5. **Awareness Match** — Is the ad messaging matched to the correct awareness level?
6. **Winning Message** — Has ONE dominant buying motivation been identified?
7. **Belief Shifts** — All 6 belief questions answered?
8. **Language Match** — Is the copy in THEIR words or YOUR words?
