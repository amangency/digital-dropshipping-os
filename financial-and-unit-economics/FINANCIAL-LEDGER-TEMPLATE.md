# 📊 Financial Ledger & Unit Economics Tracker

> **Formula Reference & Ledger Template for Digital Product Dropshipping**  
> **Payment Gateway:** Razorpay  
> **Standard Settlement Cycle:** T+1 / T+2 Business Days  
> **Standard Gateway Fee:** 2.0% + 18% GST on fee = **2.36%**  

---

## 🎯 1. Core Unit Economics Formula

For every ₹1,000 in gross revenue collected via Razorpay:

$$\text{Gross Revenue} = ₹1,000.00$$
$$\text{Razorpay Gateway Fee (2%)} = ₹20.00$$
$$\text{GST on Gateway Fee (18%)} = ₹3.60$$
$$\text{Total Deductions (2.36%)} = ₹23.60$$
$$\text{Net Bank Deposit} = ₹976.40$$

$$\text{Net Profit} = \text{Net Bank Deposit} - \text{Ad Spend}$$

## 💡 3. Key Financial Rules (Bootcamp Framework)

1. **Front-End Pays for Traffic, Backend Generates Wealth**:
   - The primary purpose of the Front-End product (₹299–₹499) + Order Bumps (₹149–₹199) is to **breakeven or produce 1.5x–2.0x ROAS on day 1 ad spend**.
   - Your pure margin / wealth comes from Step-2 Upsells (₹499–₹999) and backend coaching / community / higher-ticket bundles where **ad acquisition cost is ₹0**.
2. **Buffer for Settlement Lag**:
   - Razorpay settlements take 24–48 hours to hit your bank account (longer on bank holidays/Sundays). Always maintain a minimum ₹5,000–₹10,000 ad budget float in your bank account so your Meta ad sets don't choke while waiting for payouts.
3. **Never Spend Profit Before Net Calculations**:
   - Always calculate net profit **after** deducting GST, gateway fees, and ad spend.
