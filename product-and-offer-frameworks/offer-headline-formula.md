# Quick Reference: Offer Headline Formula

## THE FORMULA
> "How to [achieve the desired outcome] in [timeframe] without [common obstacle/fear], using [your unique mechanism] — and best of all, [additional benefit]."

## EXAMPLES

### Resume Product
"How to get shortlisted for interviews in just 7 days without rewriting your CV from scratch, using the 12-Point Recruiter Filter — and best of all, send it tonight."

### Weight Loss
"How to lose 30 kg in 30 days without heavy workouts or giving up your favourite food, using the SPT formula — and best of all, look and feel healthier."

### Digital Product Launch
"How to launch your first or next profitable digital product in just 48 hours — as a complete beginner."

## CHECKLIST FOR EVALUATION
- [ ] Outcome is specific and observable?
- [ ] Timeframe is named (7 days, 30 days, one weekend)?
- [ ] Obstacle/fear is the buyer's actual worry?
- [ ] Mechanism has a name (even if just branded)?
- [ ] Additional benefit makes it irresistible?
- [ ] Written in buyer's language, not creator's?

## COMMON MISTAKES
1. No timeframe → feels vague → low conversion
2. Generic obstacle ("without stress") → doesn't resonate
3. No mechanism name → no certainty → no differentiation
4. Too long → not memorable → can't repeat to friends
5. Feature-focused instead of outcome-focused
