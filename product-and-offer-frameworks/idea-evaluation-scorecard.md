# Quick Reference: Idea Evaluation Scorecard

## SCORING CRITERIA

### 1. Real Pain (Weight: 25%)
**Questions to ask:**
- Do people actively complain about this in communities/forums?
- Can the buyer describe this problem in their own words without prompting?
- Is it something they've tried to solve before and failed?
- Are there Facebook groups, WhatsApp groups, Reddit threads about this?

**Scoring:**
- 9-10: Massive, widespread pain with emotional charge (job loss fear, health crisis, relationship breakdown)
- 7-8: Clear pain, regularly discussed, affects daily life
- 5-6: Real but not urgent, "nice to have" territory
- 3-4: Pain exists but buyer doesn't recognise it yet (you'd have to convince them)
- 1-2: Manufactured pain, nobody actually talks about this

---

### 2. Emotional Urgency (Weight: 20%)
**Questions to ask:**
- Is there a trigger event that makes them search RIGHT NOW?
- Will they postpone to "next month" or do they need it TODAY?
- Is there a consequence of NOT acting now?

**Scoring:**
- 9-10: Life-changing urgency (job interview in 3 days, wedding coming, health scare)
- 7-8: Clear time pressure (career stagnation, seasonal opportunity)
- 5-6: Want it but won't rush (self-improvement, curiosity)
- 3-4: "Someday" category (no trigger event)
- 1-2: No urgency whatsoever

---

### 3. Willingness to Pay (Weight: 20%)
**Questions to ask:**
- Who is ALREADY selling in this exact category?
- What prices are competitors charging?
- Are there ads running in this space? (Ad Library check)
- How many paid products exist on this topic?
- What are Amazon review counts for books in this niche?

**Scoring:**
- 9-10: Multiple competitors selling, active ads, high review counts, proven demand
- 7-8: Some competitors, moderate ad activity, growing market
- 5-6: Few competitors, unclear if people pay for this specific thing
- 3-4: Almost no one selling this, "gap" that might be a warning
- 1-2: People expect this to be free, no payment history

---

### 4. Ebook Fit (Weight: 10%)
**Questions to ask:**
- Can this problem be solved in 30-40 pages with clear actions?
- Does the buyer need step-by-step instructions (ebook works) or hands-on practice (course needed)?
- Can you deliver a visible win by page 4?

**Scoring:**
- 9-10: Perfect ebook topic — clear steps, actionable, 5-8 chapter structure natural
- 7-8: Works as ebook with some creative structuring
- 5-6: Borderline — might need templates or video to be complete
- 3-4: Better suited to course, tool, or software
- 1-2: Cannot be delivered as an ebook

---

### 5. Micro-Niche Clarity (Weight: 10%)
**Questions to ask:**
- Can you name the EXACT person who buys this? (Age, profession, situation)
- Can you write "I help [specific person] [specific outcome]"?
- Is the audience narrow enough that a beginner can win?

**Scoring:**
- 9-10: Crystal clear buyer persona (e.g., "divorced Indian professionals aged 30-55")
- 7-8: Clear segment but could be narrower
- 5-6: Broad audience, trying to serve too many people
- 3-4: "Everyone" is the target
- 1-2: No idea who the buyer is

---

### 6. Speed-to-Win (Weight: 5%)
- 9-10: Result in minutes (fill template, use checklist)
- 7-8: Result in hours (read guide, implement steps)
- 5-6: Result in days
- 3-4: Result in weeks
- 1-2: Result in months

---

### 7. Product Ladder Potential (Weight: 5%)
- 9-10: Clear 3+ product progression (front → mid → high ticket)
- 7-8: Obvious next product exists
- 5-6: Possible next product but unclear
- 3-4: Dead end after first product
- 1-2: No backend opportunity

---

### 8. India Market Fit (Weight: 5%)
- 9-10: Massive Indian market, culturally relevant, payment-ready audience
- 7-8: Good Indian market, growing awareness
- 5-6: Works but needs adaptation from Western model
- 3-4: Primarily Western market, India is secondary
- 1-2: Does not work in Indian context

---

## FINAL CALCULATION
**Weighted Score = (Pain×0.25) + (Urgency×0.20) + (WTP×0.20) + (EbookFit×0.10) + (NicheClarity×0.10) + (Speed×0.05) + (Ladder×0.05) + (IndiaFit×0.05)**
